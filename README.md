🎮 From FIFA 23 Data to Football Insights — My Latest Tableau Project

What can FIFA 23 player data tell us beyond who has the highest rating?

For my latest Data Analytics & Business Intelligence portfolio project, I used FIFA 23 player data to build an interactive Tableau dashboard exploring the relationship between player performance, potential, wages, market value, and club strength.

📊 What did I find?

🔹 Club strength:
FC Bayern München ranked highest among the selected clubs by average player Overall rating, at around 80, followed closely by Paris Saint-Germain and Inter.

🔹 Current performance vs. future potential:
There is a clear positive relationship between Overall rating and Potential. However, some players with lower current ratings have significantly higher potential, showing why potential can be important when identifying future talent.

🔹 Ratings vs. wages:
Players with higher Overall ratings generally earn higher wages. However, the relationship isn't perfect — players with similar ratings can have noticeably different wages.

🔹 Market value & potential:
The Top 20 market-value players reveal an interesting mix of established stars and players with strong future potential. Kylian Mbappé leads the group with a Potential rating of 95, followed by players such as Erling Haaland (94) and Phil Foden (92).

💡 My biggest takeaway

Overall rating, Potential, Market Value, and Wages tell different parts of the same story.

A player can have an excellent current rating, but another player with a lower rating may have greater development potential. Similarly, market value and wages don't always increase proportionally with player rating.

This makes the dataset interesting not only from a football perspective, but also from a data analytics and decision-making perspective.
